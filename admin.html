Kitchen Accuracy

Every order records the time it was quoted and the time it was actually finished. This compares the two. If on-time is drifting, the numbers in eta/model need nudging — this tells you which direction, instead of guessing.

Repeat Customers

Counted from phone numbers on takeaway and delivery orders — dine-in walk-ins aren't identified, so this covers only the orders that carry a number.

Void a Bill

Reverse a settled bill — refunds, billing errors, or cancellations after payment. The sale is reversed in the till and a visible reversal line is written to the ledger; the original bill is kept in history, flagged as voided. Admin only.
